03 — The turnover standard

What a full turnover actually includes.

No vague promises. This is the checklist every Elixir turnover follows, verified by a final visual inspection before your guests arrive.

  • Whole-home ventilation for fresh air and airflow
  • Full inspection for damage, missing items & lost property
  • Garbage, recycling and composting removed
  • Fresh linens, beds styled to your listing’s look
  • Towels refreshed, folded and staged
  • All laundry washed, dried and stored
  • High-touch surfaces disinfected, Health Canada approved
  • Kitchen reset — dishes, appliances and surfaces
  • Bathroom sanitised, mirrors and glass spotless
  • Floors swept, vacuumed and mopped to your preference
  • Guest supplies restocked — soap, coffee, tea, water
  • Final visual inspection before every check-in

Optional extras — guest gifts, handwritten welcome notes, carpet and upholstery care — can be added to any package.
